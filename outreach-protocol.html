THE 4-STAGE DOUBLE-BLIND PROTOCOL.

How our senior headhunters execute zero-footprint outreach without exposing candidate identities or enterprise plans.

Review Our Security Architecture Initiate Private Mandate
4
Controlled stages
01

Confidential Mandate Intake

Board Chair or Founder briefs a Senior Partner directly under signed NDA. No job description is ever posted publicly; the mandate exists only inside Zeal Employer's encrypted intake system.

02

Anonymized Market Mapping

Senior Partners identify and approach qualified sitting executives using coded mandate profiles — company identity, compensation band, and strategic context are disclosed only in general terms until mutual interest is confirmed.

03

Double-Blind Qualification

Shortlisted candidates and the hiring entity are evaluated against each other's criteria without either party's identity being revealed. Interviews are conducted through secure, firm-mediated channels.

04

Mutual Disclosure & Closing

Once both sides formally consent in writing, full identities are exchanged, final negotiations are facilitated by the Senior Partner, and the placement is closed under continued confidentiality through the transition period.
